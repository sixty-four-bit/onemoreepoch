About

One more epoch.

In machine learning, an epoch is one full pass over the training data — the model doesn't get everything the first time through, so you run it again, each pass sharpening the picture a little more. That's the idea behind the show: one more pass over what's happening in AI.

Why we made this

There's plenty of content that covers AI from the outside — the funding rounds, the hype cycles, the hot takes. We wanted something different.

OneMoreEpoch is built for people who want the real mechanics: how models are trained, what agents can and can't do yet, and what a paper actually found once you strip out the headline.

We cover the stuff that's genuinely moving — not because it's trending, but because it's where the most interesting problems live right now.

What we stand for

Technical depth

We don't shy away from the details. If there's a paper, a benchmark, or a war story behind the decision, we want to hear it.

No hype cycle

Every model has real limits. We talk about what doesn't work as much as what does.

Primary sources

We read the papers and run the demos ourselves before we talk about them on the show.
